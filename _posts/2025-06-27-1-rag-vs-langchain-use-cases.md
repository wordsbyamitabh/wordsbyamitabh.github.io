What are Large Language Models (LLMs)?

Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI systems designed to understand and generate human language. They leverage vast datasets to learn context, grammar, and semantics, enabling them to produce coherent and contextually relevant text. Their applications range from chat models like chatbots to content creation.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Explained

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is a way to mix the power of large language models with a strong information retrieval system. This works by using a vector database to find relevant documents and relevant information when there is a user query. The language model then uses these documents, often broken down into smaller chunks, to provide additional information to answer the user's question. This can give new knowledge for the final response from external sources.

This way of using information retrieval means that RAG can give specific information in its final response. It takes unstructured data, breaks it into smaller parts, and uses what is most useful when a person asks something. The language model and the retrieval step come together, so the answer you get will fit your user queries better, and it will be more complete. This helps to make the most of both generation and retrieval for the user.
